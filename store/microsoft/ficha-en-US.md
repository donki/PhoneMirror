## Description

```
sOC Phone Mirror shows your Android phone's screen in a Windows window and lets you control it from
the PC: touch with the mouse, type with the keyboard and drag files onto it.

Plug the phone in by USB, press connect, done. The window follows the phone: open a game or a video
in landscape and the window turns landscape with it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Tap, swipe and scroll with the mouse; right button for back, middle button for home.
• Type with the PC keyboard, with your own keyboard layout.
• Copy and paste both ways (Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V).
• Buttons for back, home, recents, notifications, screen off, rotate and volume.
• Save a screenshot of the phone's screen with one click.
• Drag an APK to install it, or any file to copy it to the phone.
• One window per phone if you have several connected.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR PC
Everything travels over the USB cable between the PC and the phone. No server, no account, nothing
goes to the internet. No ads, no trackers, no analytics.

WHAT IT NEEDS
An Android phone or tablet (5.0 or later) with USB debugging enabled, and the Android platform-tools
(adb) on the PC. The app looks for them in the usual places.

Free software under the MIT licence. Spanish and English, light and dark mode.
```
